## 🚀 Choose Your Version

WMAROS offers two optimization paths depending on your hardware and data preservation needs.

### 💎 Option 1: WMAROS ULTRA (Recommended)
> **Focus:** Absolute maximum performance.
> **Warning:** Requires a complete drive format (Clean Install).

#### Prerequisites:
* **Bases:** 
>Windows 11 25H2 (WMAROS11U)  
>Windows 10 22H2(WMAROS10U).
* **Tool:** USB Flash Drive (8GB+) and [Rufus](https://rufus.ie/).

#### Step-by-Step:
1. **Download:** Select your version W10 or W11 and get the official ISO from the [releases](https://github.com/alrigroup/wmaros/releases).
2. **Preparation:** Use Rufus to flash the ISO to your USB drive (GPT partition scheme recommended for UEFI).
3. **Boot:** Boot your PC from the USB drive (adjust boot priority in BIOS).
4. **Installation:** Format the destination drive and proceed with the **WMAROS1XU** installation.
   * *Note: Command prompt windows may appear during the process. Do not close them; this is the automated AROS setup at work.*
5. **Post-Installation:** 
   * Download the **Optimization Program** from the [releases](https://github.com/alrigroup/wmaros/releases).
   * Extract the content to your Desktop.
   * Run `AME Beta.exe` and select the `WMAROS.apbx` file to apply final tweaks.
6. **Finalization:** The system may reboot several times. Once finished, restart manually one last time.

---

### ⚡ Option 2: WMAROS LITE
> **Focus:** Optimization without losing personal files.
> **Efficiency:** Approximately 70%–80% of the Ultra version's performance.

#### Supported Versions:
* **Windows 10:** 1809, 20H2, 21H2, 22H2.
* **Windows 11:** 21H2, 22H2, 23H2, 24H2, 25H2.

#### Step-by-Step:
1. **Download:** Get the official **AME BETA.exe and WMAROS.apbx** in [releases](https://github.com/alrigroup/wmaros/releases).
2. **Preparation:** 
   * Extract the files to your Desktop.
   * **Important:** Temporarily disable Windows Defender/Antivirus.
   * Using a **Local Account** is highly recommended to avoid sync conflicts.
3. **Execution:** Run `AME Beta.exe` and load the `WMAROS.apbx` file.
4. **Processing:** The system will apply optimizations and may reboot automatically.
5. **Done:** Experience the difference and enjoy your optimized PC.
